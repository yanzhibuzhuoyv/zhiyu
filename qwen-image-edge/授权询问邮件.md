Dear Qwen Team,

I am building an **on-device (Android/iOS) text-to-image application** based on **Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo**,
and I would like to clarify the licensing terms before investing further engineering effort.

**What the application does**
- Runs entirely on the user's phone (no server-side inference).
- Only the text-to-image function is used; editing, multi-reference and the prompt-rewriter models are not shipped.
- The model weights are quantized (int4/int8) and possibly have the unused `lm_head` and vision tower removed.
- In one planned configuration, the text encoder runs on our server and only the 7B DiT + VAE run on the device.

**Distribution model**
- Distributed **free of charge**, with **no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no paid tiers**.
- Not bundled into any commercial product; no revenue is derived from it.
- Source code intended to be published openly.

**Questions**
1. Does free, non-commercial distribution to the general public fall under the Qwen Research License

2. If a separate license is required, what is the process and the typical commercial terms for
   an on-device distribution of this kind?
3. Are modified/quantized/pruned redistributions of the weights permitted under the same terms,
   and what attribution or notice must be preserved?

## 备选：不想等授权就走 Apache-2.0 路线

如果要"发给别人用"且不想承担授权不确定性，可直接换 **Apache-2.0** 的端侧优先模型：

| 模型 | 参数量 | 步数 | 许可 | 端侧先例 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Z-Image-Turbo | 6B | 8 | **Apache-2.0** | LiteRT / stable-diffusion.cpp / local-dream |
| FLUX.2 klein | 4B | 4 | **Apache-2.0** | LiteRT + QNN |
| SANA 1.5 | 1.6B | 5-8 | **Apache-2.0**（文本编码器受 Gemma 条款） | MNN 官方 demo |

代价：放弃 Qwen-Image 的**中文/图内文字渲染**优势 —— 这恰好是这个项目最初的动机，所以先问授权更划算。
